# Finance app UX audit (21 Nov 2026)

What I looked at: the installed app (~/Applications/Finance.app, real data, read-only: I opened tabs and disclosures and changed nothing), plus the devserver running on a copy of the ledger so I could click buttons that write. I also read the tokens in `app/ui/styles`.

Severity: **P0** means the numbers are wrong or contradict each other, so you can't trust the screen. **P1** means it confuses you or slows you down every day. **P2** is polish.

---

## P0: numbers that disagree or mislead

1. **Wiggle room disagrees between pages.** Overview → Spendable shows "Wiggle room €0.00" (in green) and "To wishes €597.63". Plan → Where the money goes shows "Wiggle room €597.63". It's the same idea with two values.
2. **The card bill is invisible.** Wishes → Credit cards says "Used this statement €575.98", but the Plan bill "Credit card" is **€0.00 monthly, 2 Nov**. The €576 charge on 1–2 Dec doesn't appear in Next 7 days, Spendable, Coming up or Funds December. This is the largest real cash outflow, and the app hides it. "Used this statement" is also typed in by hand when it could be summed from the 26th–25th card transactions.
3. **Over-budget events read as fine.** Events → "Parents in NL" has a €200 budget and €794 used, yet it shows **100%**, a full blue bar, an "Affordable" badge and "Extra to find €0". It should read 397%, €594 over, in the negative colour, with the bar overflowing.
4. **Three versions of "how much is left".** The header says Left in plan €140.36 and Month end vs plan **+€431.79** in blue. Spending trajectory says Spent €1,480 against a €1,256 budget and "vs budget **+€491.59**", also in blue. A plus sign in the same blue means "good" in one place and "over" in another. Unplanned spending is −€154.50 on Plan, while Overview shows €202.59 still expected.
5. **Goal savings contradict.** Wishes shows "Saved for wishes €400", but every wish card says "€0 saved 0%". Wishes marks Japan "Affordable", while Spending → Goals says Japan is "Short €1,155".
6. **The balance projection starts at €0** on 21 Nov, but the real balance is about €1.2k. Either the chart shows cumulative net flow and the label "Balance projection" is wrong, or the line is off. The headline also shows two numbers ("Following your plan €5,507.87", "Final balance €5,695.06") with no explanation of which one to read.
7. **The month labels mix periods.** It is 21 Nov, yet the Do-this-now tiles say "December, a week", "December visits", next to "Spendable · November" and "Funds December". The payday period (26th) is leaking into the labels. Name it once ("Pay month 26 Nov – 25 Dec") and use the calendar month everywhere else.
8. **"−€0.00"** appears in Spendable (Savings, Salary set aside). Zero has no sign.

## P1: colour semantics (the biggest design fix)

- `--ds-over` is blue (hue 262), almost the same as `--ds-accent` (hue 266) used for links ("Edit plan →", "Habits →"), Groceries, the "Habits" forecast bars and the "Following plan" line. Over-budget, positive surplus, links and categories all share one blue. **Fix:** over and short use `--ds-negative` (the existing warm red-orange, hue 35). Surplus uses `--ds-positive`. Blue is kept for interactive elements only. This reverses the "blue for over-amounts" choice from #114. Blue reads as calm or neutral in finance, which is the wrong signal for overspending.
- Green is used for €0.00 ("Wiggle room €0.00") and for "Current habits" in the projection, where the habits line ends lower, which is bad.
- Category swatches collide: Groceries and Food & drinks out are both blue; Saved for wishes and To wishes are both teal. Give each budget group a fixed hue and reuse it in every chart.
- Status pills (Over pace, Over limit, Over usual month, Faster, Plan at risk, Needs attention, Above norm, Card bill short, Short) all use the same lavender pill. Seven severities look identical. Use three tones: neutral, warning (amber) and danger (red). Cap the vocabulary at about five words.
- Payment boxes use strikethrough for parts that are paid. Strikethrough means *cancelled*. Use a check mark and muted text instead.

## P1: too much, and in too many places

- **Overview is the whole app.** It runs about 14 screens of scrolling: KPI strip, Do this now, Spendable plus Funds, Monthly budget, 7-day forecast, 3-month projection, Habit score, Raise your score, Are you on track, Trajectory, Budget table, Extra income, Trends. Several blocks repeat:
  - Planned / Spent / Short / In savings / Savings by month end appears **3 times** (KPI strip, Monthly budget, Are-you-on-track).
  - Habit score 50/100 appears twice. Spendable €0.00 appears twice.
  - "Where the money goes" plus "Short for bills and budgets" appears in full on **Plan and Manage → Close month**.

  Proposal: Overview = one hero answer ("Safe to spend until 26 Nov: €X/day"), Do this now, 7 days and the budget table. Move the projection and trends to Spending. Delete the duplicate blocks.
- **Do this now** has 7 tiles, and 4 of them say "Stop for this month". Repeated identical commands cause alarm fatigue. Collapse them into one "Over budget in 4 groups (€839)" tile with a list. Also, "Entertainment €221 over usual month" sits next to "December €7.00", and nothing on the tile explains how €7 relates to €221.
- **Spending opens on an empty screen.** The default sub-tab is "Organise categories", filtered to "Needs a group", which gives "0 / 0, No categories match this view". The Organise/Review toggle repeats the sub-tabs. Counts disagree too: "Grouped 83" vs "117 of 117 grouped". Default to Habits & goals, and hide Organise behind a badge that appears only when something needs sorting.
- **Settings are spread across pages.** Credit-card limits and the Emergency fund config live in Wishes. Account roles (Spending/Savings/Don't track) live in Close month. Salary appears in Settings and under the projection ("Edit salary"). Move all of them to Manage → Settings.
- **Closed and stale things still show:** "Trade Republic (closed)" appears in account pickers and health checks, "Headphones" is offered as a savings goal, "Parents in NL €0/€0/€0" rows appear in the budget table, and empty "Late" and "To check" kanban columns take up full width. Hide zero rows and empty columns.
- **The category picker** is a flat list of about 130 entries that mixes merchants (Jumbo, Lidl), categories (Groceries) and **people's names** (4 private individuals). It needs grouping (group → category), search, and recent items first. Merchants should be a separate field from category.
- **Transactions** defaults to all time (3,627 rows, IN €74k / OUT €75k), which doesn't answer any question. Default to this month. Group rows by day with day totals, colour income green, and show the raw SEPA string only on expand.

## P1: interaction bugs found while clicking

- **The sticky tab bar collides with the macOS traffic lights.** After a short scroll it slides under the close, minimise and zoom buttons, and clicks on "Overview" then hit the title-bar drag region. Further down the page it disappears completely. On Transactions it doesn't stick at all. Pick one behaviour: sticky, offset by the title-bar inset (about 28px), with a solid backing.
- **Esc doesn't close popups** (Plan payment box). The page behind keeps scrolling too.
- **Bill boxes don't open, but one-off boxes do**, even though the hint on the page says "Open a box for its details". The cards also aren't reachable by keyboard (they're `<div>`s in the a11y tree).
- **"8 more, less often"** on Spending → Habits did nothing when clicked in the prod app.
- **The amount column wraps:** "−€750.00" breaks onto two lines in Transactions. Use `whitespace-nowrap` and tabular figures.
- **The category select truncates** ("Albert Heijn T", "Kruidva", "Interne") at 1024px.
- **The active tab highlight lags** behind the click. During loading the old tab stays selected while skeletons show.
- **Add plan** with empty fields silently focuses Name and shows no message. Add plan and Add wish look like inputs or secondary buttons, while Save cards and Save fund are solid black. The primary action style is inconsistent.
- **The Plan payment popup** puts the values in half the width with the right half empty, has no edit option, makes "Remove" the only (grey text) action, and doesn't show which transaction paid it.

## P1: personal-finance logic and IA (expert view)

- **Lead with one number.** Good PFM apps (YNAB "Ready to assign", Copilot "Safe to spend") answer *"what can I spend today?"*. Here the first card says "Spendable now €0.00 / Savings cover it / Short €13.73 / Take from savings €13.73", which is four facts and no verdict. Suggested hero: **"€X a day until payday (26 Nov, 5 days)"**, with a single status of on track, tight or over.
- **Separate commitment from forecast.** Planned bills (certain), budgets (intent) and the unplanned estimate (statistical) are summed together in "≈ €334.64" and similar figures. Show certain money in solid style and estimates in a lighter style with a range. You already compute ranges (€145–€399), so show them instead of a point value with ≈.
- **Habit score (50/100) with point penalties** is gamification that punishes without guiding. "Raise your score" repeats the Do-this-now advice. Keep the concrete advice ("€40 max for the next 3 days") and drop the score, or show it as a trend.
- **Close month** has 4 steps but no progress across steps, and the October closure sits next to November balances. Make it a real wizard: a step indicator, Next/Back, and a summary at the end.
- **Month by month (Wishes)** shows 12 cards with 6 numbers each. It should be one line chart (free money and savings over time), with a warning when savings hit €0. Today it quietly shows "Savings after €0.00" from December onwards with no alert.
- **The goal table** has a "Short on budget" column that is "—" in every row. Drop it.
- **Dates come in 4 formats:** `2026-11-20`, `20/11/2026, 16:56:36`, `21 Nov`, `25 Sept 2027`. The date inputs show `dd/mm/yyyy` in the devserver and US `11/21/2026` in the prod WebView. Use one helper: "21 Nov" in the current year, "21 Nov 2025" otherwise, and no seconds anywhere.

## P2: visual and design-system details

- **Contrast.** `--ds-fg-muted` (L 0.51) on the glass page (about L 0.86 grey in prod) gives roughly 3:1. It is used for nearly all 12px labels and values ("Planned", "Short", table sub-lines), so it fails WCAG AA (4.5:1). Darken muted to about L 0.45, or use it only at 14px and up.
- **Type scale.** 118× `text-xs` (12px), plus ad-hoc `text-[11px]`, `text-[10px]`, `text-[0.65rem]` and `text-[0.68rem]`. The uppercase tracked micro-labels at 10–11px ("SPENDABLE NOW", "BALANCE · €1,118.33") are the hardest text to read on the page. Set a floor of 12px (11px only for all-caps labels), and drop the arbitrary sizes in favour of 5 steps: 11 caps, 13 body-sm, 15 body, 20, 32.
- **Opacity.** `opacity-50/60/45/75` is used for disabled and secondary states, along with alpha colours /10–/90. On glass, low-opacity text picks up the background tint and loses more contrast. Use solid muted tokens instead of opacity on text.
- **Glass load.** A backdrop blur of 18–34px is applied to every input, select, secondary button and card. Pages like Transactions have hundreds of blurred selects, which makes scrolling heavier and turns every row's control into a frosted blob. Keep glass for the window, tab bar and popups, and give cards and fields solid fills.
- **Layout.** Label–value pairs are spread across the full width (e.g. "From Revolut to ABN AMRO …… €78.37" with about 700px between them), which makes them hard to scan. Cap row width at about 480px or use a 2-column table. The KPI strip packs 7 equal columns with no hierarchy. The 7-day forecast chart uses half its row. The Trends bars are thin and far apart, and "Nov*" has an unexplained asterisk.
- **Tables.** Amount columns need `tabular-nums`, right alignment and no wrapping. Remove sub-lines that repeat the title ("Jumbo Stephensonlaan / Jumbo Stephensonlaan").
- **Disclosure styling** mixes "► More" text triangles, chevrons ( › ) and pill buttons ("8 more, less often"). Use one component.
- **Copy.** It is wordy and system-centred in places ("Drag one to Paid to name the payment that paid it", "Evaluation hour", "IMPORT / WRITE"). It also has typos in the UI copy: "Accomodation", "Parent's accomodation" (user data, but the category name is app-wide).

## Suggested implementation order (one session)

1. Colour semantics: over/short → negative token, blue only for actions, three-tone pills. This is mostly tokens and Tone mapping.
2. Fix the P0 numbers: card bill from the statement, event over-percentage, wiggle-room source of truth, signed-zero formatting, month-period labels.
3. Remove duplicates and slim Overview down. Default Spending to Habits. Hide zero rows and empty columns. Move config to Settings.
4. Interaction bugs: sticky bar inset, Esc to close plus keyboard-focusable boxes, nowrap amounts, the "8 more" button, consistent primary buttons, empty-form validation message.
5. Type, contrast and glass tokens: muted darker, size floor, solid cards and fields.
6. Transactions defaults (this month, group by day), grouped category picker, one date format.

Not done: dark mode and phone width weren't checked. Import, Close-month steps 1–3 and the email/AI settings were only read, not exercised. The P0 numbers come from screens, and I haven't traced their calculations in `crates/core` yet.
