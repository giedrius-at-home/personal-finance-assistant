# Events Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A wish can carry event dates; it then owns the day-to-day spending of those days, is judged on the extra over the owner's usual days, is paced while it runs, and shows on its own Events tab, on Overview and on Plan.

**Architecture:** An event is a stored `Wish` with an optional `event` field, so the wish table, writes and ladder are reused. A small pure module (`crates/core/src/events.rs`) decides which transactions an event claims and does the day arithmetic. `budget::Ownership` asks it first, so groups and habits stop seeing event days; the groups hand over their usual share of those days; what the event still needs enters the existing wish ladder as one demand per calendar month.

**Tech Stack:** Rust workspace: `finance-types`, `finance-core`, `finance-storage` (untouched), Tauri (`app/src-tauri`), devserver (`app/devserver`), Leptos (`app/ui`). `rust_decimal`, `jiff`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-20-events-design.md`, part 3. Read it first.

## The arithmetic, once

For an event wish `E` (top-level, one-off, `event` set), in its currency:

- `rest` = cap − planned parts − bought parts: the existing `Family::rest` (a wish without parts: its amount).
- *owned* transactions = hand-picked ∪ (dated in `[starts, ends]` ∧ day-to-day), minus excluded. Day-to-day: `budget::counts`, not in `fixed_ids`, not the transaction of a paid planned payment, reviewed group not `Transfers`/`Investments`.
- *tied* = owned transactions named by a bought part's `WishPurchase.transaction_id`; their money is the part's, counted once, there.
- `picked_ahead` = Σ owned, untied, dated before `starts`. `days_money` = `rest − picked_ahead` (≥ 0).
- `days` = ends − starts + 1; `days_m` = event days in calendar month `m`.
- `share_m` = `days_money × days_m ÷ days` (2 dp, the last month takes the rounding).
- `spent_m` = Σ owned, untied, dated from `starts` on, in budget month `m` (`budget::month_of`).
- `usual_m` = Σ over day-to-day groups of `usual(group) × days_m ÷ days_in_month(m)`, `usual` = `wishes::goals::usual` over the group's earlier history months (event transactions already out).
- **Group release:** in month `m` each day-to-day group reserves `min(its usual share, what it would reserve)` less. That is the whole "usual" mechanism: wiggle room rises by the released money, and the event then asks for its full share from it.
- **Demand:** month `m` asks `max(0, share_m − spent_m)`, zero for months before the running one and after `ends`. Money saved for the event pays the earliest month first.
- Shown: *Usual those days* = Σ `usual_m`; *Extra to find* = `max(0, days_money − Σ usual_m)`; *Spent* = bought parts + all owned untied; *Left* = amount − planned parts still unbought − Spent, i.e. `rest − owned untied`; *A day, to stay inside* = Left ÷ days left (today included); pace on `days_money` against Σ `spent_m`.

## Review Focus

1. An event wholly in the past: claims its transactions for the history months, demands nothing, releases nothing in the running month.
2. A refund inside the dates lowers `spent_m`; it never makes a demand exceed `share_m`.
3. A transaction both hand-picked and excluded, or picked into two events: excluded wins; then the first event by start date, then by `wish_id`.
4. Usual share larger than the planned amount (a cheap visit): extra is zero, the groups release no more than `days_money` in total, and nothing goes negative.
5. An event starting today with `ends < starts` or a currency without an allocation: rejected on save; unjudged (not a panic) in the view.
